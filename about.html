About RADbuild

Infrastructure is part of the imaging system.

RADbuild is the radiology infrastructure brand of ADS Infra Solutions Private Limited. We focus on the specialised environment around diagnostic imaging equipment — helping owners and project teams plan, coordinate and execute the spaces required for modern imaging.

Our focus

Technical thinking. Practical execution.

A successful imaging room depends on much more than the scanner. Access, room geometry, shielding, electrical systems, HVAC, equipment interfaces and installation sequencing all need to work together.

RADbuild brings these considerations into one coordinated project approach, with ADS Infra Solutions Private Limited supporting execution and project management.

ADS INFRA SOLUTIONS / RADBUILDBuilt around the equipment.
Leadership

Led by a qualified civil engineer.

RADbuild is led by a qualified civil engineer with experience working with global real estate firms including JLL, and turnkey project management experience gained at GE Healthcare.

That combination is the reason the approach works. The structural, civil and building-services judgement comes from the engineering side. The understanding of how imaging equipment is specified, scheduled and signed off on site comes from having worked inside that industry — which is exactly where most infrastructure contractors are guessing.
