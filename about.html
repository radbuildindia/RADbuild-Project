About RADbuild

Infrastructure is part of the imaging system.

RADbuild is the radiology infrastructure brand of ADS Infrasolution Pvt Ltd. We focus on the specialised environment around diagnostic imaging equipment — helping owners and project teams plan, coordinate and execute the spaces required for modern imaging.

Our focus

Technical thinking. Practical execution.

A successful imaging room depends on much more than the scanner. Access, room geometry, shielding, electrical systems, HVAC, equipment interfaces and installation sequencing all need to work together.

RADbuild brings these considerations into one coordinated project approach, with ADS Infrasolution Pvt Ltd supporting execution and project management.
