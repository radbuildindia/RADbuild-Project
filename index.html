What we solve

The questions nobody warns you about.

Most diagnostic centre projects do not fail on the scanner. They fail on the things around it — discovered late, when they are expensive to fix.

01

Can the equipment get in?

The full delivery route from gate to gantry position — corridors, lifts, door widths, turning radii — checked against the actual machine before you commit to a site. This is the one that stops projects late.

Learn more →
02

Will the structure take it?

Slab and beam capacity checked against magnet, gantry and ceiling-mounted loads. Where an older building falls short, we design and build the strengthening so the premises can still be used.

Learn more →
03

Lead doors that don't leak

Correctly lapped lead sheet, frames and hinges sized for the real door weight, sealed interfaces, then leakage-surveyed on completion. A lead door is an engineered assembly, not a carpentry job.
