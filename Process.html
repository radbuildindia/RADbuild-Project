Our process

A structured path from site to ready room.

Imaging projects involve multiple stakeholders and specialist interfaces. A clear sequence helps reduce avoidable surprises during execution.

01 / ASSESS

Site Assessment

Understand the building, access, services, room constraints and equipment requirements.

02 / DESIGN

Plan

Develop layouts and coordinate technical requirements across the room and its interfaces.

03 / BUILD

Execute

Coordinate civil, MEP, shielding and specialist works against the project plan.

04 / INSTALL

Coordinate

Prepare the environment for equipment delivery, positioning and installation.

05 / READY

Handover

Close out coordination items and support final readiness for operations.

RADBUILD / DELIVERYOne coordinated workflow.
Project coordination

Keep the room, services and equipment moving together.

The goal is not simply to complete construction. It is to create an environment that is ready for the imaging equipment and the teams who will use it.

  • Define responsibilities and interfaces early
  • Coordinate specialist vendors and contractors
  • Track room readiness against equipment requirements
  • Identify access and sequencing constraints before they become site issues
  • Keep stakeholders aligned through execution
