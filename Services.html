Services

Everything around the scanner needs to work.

RADbuild coordinates the infrastructure and project interfaces that make diagnostic imaging spaces practical, safe and ready for equipment installation.

01

Site Feasibility

Review of room dimensions, structural conditions, access, equipment movement, utilities and project constraints to identify issues before execution.

02

Room Planning & Design

Planning of imaging rooms, control rooms, equipment areas, patient flow and interfaces based on the selected equipment and site.

03

Radiation Shielding Coordination

Coordination of shielding requirements and construction interfaces for relevant X-ray and CT environments, aligned with project and consultant requirements.

04

RF Shielding & MRI Interfaces

Coordination of RF shielding, equipment-room interfaces and associated services for MRI environments, alongside specialist vendors where required.

05

MEP & Building Services Coordination

Integration of electrical, HVAC, plumbing, fire and other building services with the imaging equipment and room requirements.

06

Project Management & Execution

Coordination of contractors, specialist vendors, schedules, site readiness and installation interfaces through project handover.
