Services

Four disciplines, one accountable team

Most delays on imaging builds happen at the seams — between the civil contractor, the shielding vendor, and the OEM install team. We run all four as one continuous scope.

01 / FEASIBILITY

Site & Feasibility

Structural load, floor-to-floor clearance, power and chiller capacity, fringe-field mapping, and AERB feasibility review before you sign a lease.

02 / DESIGN

Shielding & Engineering

RF and radiation shielding design, HVAC and cryogen venting, layout drawings sized to NCRP-147 and AERB requirements, ready for regulator submission.

03 / PROCUREMENT

Vendor Selection

OEM comparison and RFPs, shielding and HVAC subcontractor sourcing, AMC negotiation — vetted against your budget and your timeline, not theirs.

04 / DELIVERY

Project Management

One master schedule across civil, shielding, and OEM install. We hold the site-readiness date the OEM actually needs, and chase it.

Modality Coverage

Every modality has its own physics — and its own failure points

We design to the constraint that actually governs each room, not a generic diagnostic-suite template.

MRI
Magnetic Resonance
RF Faraday shielding, 5-gauss fringe field exclusion zone, quench pipe routing, magnet floor loading.
CT
Computed Tomography
Lead-lined shielding sized to workload & occupancy, control booth sightlines, tube cooling.
CATH
Cardiac Cathlab
Continuous-beam shielding load, OR-adjacent HVAC if hybrid, emergency backup power.
PET-CT
Positron Emission Tomography
Radiopharmacy hot lab, AERB licensing, dose uptake room shielding, decay storage.
SPECT-CT
Single-Photon Emission CT
Hot lab required, lighter shielding load than PET, same AERB pathway.

Process

From site visit to physicist sign-off

The same sequence, run for every modality — compressed or extended depending on whether you're fitting out a new build or retrofitting an existing floor.

A

Site & Feasibility Survey

We visit the site, assess structure, power, and access, and flag anything that would block AERB approval or OEM installation before you commit.

B

Shielding & Layout Design

Room drawings and shielding calculations prepared for regulator submission and OEM technical review, in parallel.

C

Vendor & Equipment Selection

We run RFPs against your budget and case volume, and negotiate the AMC terms most practices sign without reading closely.

D

Construction & Coordination

One schedule, one point of contact, across the civil contractor, shielding crew, and OEM installation team.

E

Commissioning & Sign-off

Acceptance testing and medical physicist radiation survey, so the room is licensed and billing from day one.
